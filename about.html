ABOUT THE UNVANQUISHED.

We’re a community of gamers who enjoy spending time together—in New Eden and beyond.

WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN ONE GAME.

The Unvanquished is a gaming community built around the people we play with. EVE Online is where many of us spend our time—mining, manufacturing, and working on our own industrial goals.

We play other games together too. As the community grows, we’d like to organize more activities and give interested members more opportunities to explore and take part in PvP. What matters now is having a group we enjoy logging in with.
